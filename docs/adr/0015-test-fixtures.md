# Test fixtures: synthetic images plus owned or CC0 photos

The repo is public, so test images must be redistributable.

- **Gate tests** (hashes, unit math) use small synthetic fixtures generated in code: gradients, hard edges, thin lines, small spots.
- **Metric threshold tests** (ADR-0008) use photos the author owns or that are CC0, committed with their source and license noted next to them.

The Linux Mint wallpapers used during prototyping have mixed licenses and stay out of the repo.
